LocalCam 0.8 - DataKai - full phone framing and pipeline diagnostics

UPDATE BOTH APPS
Windows 0.8.1 hotfix fixes narrow-strip portrait clipping when rotation and mirror combine. If Android is already 0.8, only close and replace/reopen the Windows app for this hotfix.
Install artifacts/LocalCam-Android-debug.apk over your previous Android version.
Open artifacts/desktop/LocalCam.Desktop.exe.
Android now opens a welcome page. Tap Get started, then choose Wi-Fi or USB. Camera permission is requested after that choice.
IP, key and code fields have been removed from the phone UI.

WI-FI
Choose Wi-Fi on both devices. On Windows choose the address on your shared network.
Tap Scan PC QR on Android. Scanning connects automatically.
Same local network is required; internet is not.
Allow private-network TCP 8788 if Windows Firewall blocks the connection.
Guest network isolation can prevent connections.

USB
Connect a USB data cable. Enable Android USB debugging and authorize the computer.
Choose USB in Windows and Android. Click Connect Android over USB in Windows.
LocalCam runs adb reverse and opens a confirmation on the phone. Tap Allow connection only if you initiated it on the PC you trust.
No address/key/code entry is needed.
This prototype needs Android platform-tools on the PC; the development machine already has them.
The consumer USB transport and installer are later work.

SOURCE FRAMING
Tap Fullscreen in the camera controls or preview to view the phone camera without controls, including in landscape. Tap Exit fullscreen or press Android Back to restore controls.
Android defaults to Full phone view. Keep it selected to send the full preview region.
Windows defaults to Source + Fit + crop zoom 1x. Old framing preferences reset once on upgrade.
Portrait remains portrait; turn the phone horizontally for landscape. Black bars preserve all the content.
Optional Crop to... modes on Android and Fill/zoom on Windows deliberately remove edges.
Both CameraX preview and capture share a viewport and requested resolution.

PERFORMANCE
Default capture target is 640 x 360 at 30 fps with balanced JPEG quality.
Android encodes YUV directly, avoiding RGBA conversion and rotated bitmap allocations.
Where supported, it asks the camera for the closest supported sensor FPS range.
Windows never re-encodes frames for view settings; native/OBS rendering handles fit, fill, mirror, rotation and zoom.
A fixed 30 fps cannot be guaranteed on every device, lighting condition or Wi-Fi network.
Android shows capture/sent FPS, pack/rotate/JPEG milliseconds, last send time, Mbps and queued drops.
Windows shows received and preview FPS. A 30 fps conversion budget is about 33 ms per frame.
720p has four times the pixels of 360p; camera preview does not perform JPEG encoding or network sending.
Check the timing readout to distinguish phone conversion from transport or Windows rendering limits.
Full phone view now uses JPEG orientation metadata instead of rotating every frame's pixels. Update both apps and refresh OBS. Explicit crop modes still transform pixels.
Test 360p first, then 720p. Keep the phone cool and the app foreground.
Keep-screen-awake is enabled while streaming by default.

1080P AND FUTURE FEATURES
Adaptive compression is enabled by default. It adjusts JPEG quality between 40 and your chosen ceiling to reduce processing/network pressure, without changing resolution or target FPS.
Watch the actual JPEG percentage in the phone's live readout. Disable Adaptive compression if you need fixed quality.
Sustained physical 1080p30 still needs your device test; hardware encoding remains planned.
4K is visible but disabled. Blur and direct Windows webcam are also clearly labelled future features.
Microphone audio is Off / Planned and Studio mode is Planned. Neither captures audio nor starts a second camera. Audio sync/playback and hardware-dependent concurrent cameras need a separate implementation before they are usable.
Privacy information is available on the Android welcome page and the separately maintained product website.
SECURITY.txt lists release preparation and the remaining Play Store gates. This is still a development build.
